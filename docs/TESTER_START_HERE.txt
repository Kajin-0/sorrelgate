SORRELGATE — MAC TEST (VENTURA AND NEWER)
======================================

This test kit contains Sorrelgate.app and three fictional PDF samples.
It is designed for macOS 13 Ventura or newer, on Intel or Apple Silicon.
No development tools, source code, Terminal, or coding knowledge are needed.
The application has not yet been launched or certified on Ventura.

INSTALL THE APP
1. Double-click the downloaded ZIP to extract the Sorrelgate folder.
2. Open the extracted folder, locate Sorrelgate.app and copy it into
   your Applications folder. Do not overwrite a previous version without
   choosing to replace it intentionally.
3. Double-click Sorrelgate in Applications to try opening it.
4. This early test app is NOT signed or notarized by Apple.
   If macOS reports it cannot verify the developer, only if you trust
   the privately supplied app and wish to proceed: open Apple menu >
   System Settings > Privacy & Security, find the blocked Sorrelgate
   app near the Security section, and select Open Anyway, then
   confirm the app-specific decision. Apple may request your password.
   Never disable Gatekeeper or other system-wide protections.
   If macOS warns that the software is damaged or malicious, STOP.
5. If the app still cannot open, record the exact warning and stop.
   You can ask for help in a web browser; no desktop assistant is needed.

TEST ONLY WITH THE INCLUDED FICTIONAL DOCUMENTS
1. Create an Accepted Offer transaction at "123 Example Lane".
2. Import the three PDFs in "Sample Documents".
3. Open Review and select "Find details in PDFs".
4. Open an original source page to check suggested dates and prices.
   Mark one suggestion "Not sure".
5. Open Documents to inspect a PDF and Checklist to finish one task.
6. Export to a new folder. Quit and reopen Sorrelgate to check that
   the transaction, documents and decisions remain saved.
7. Record what succeeded or failed in "TEST RESULTS.txt".

No real client contracts, private details, or licensed forms in this
initial test. Sorrelgate cannot establish legal compliance, send
messages, or automatically act as an AI operator.

The included SHA-256 manifests can confirm that files in this kit have
not changed, but they are NOT an Apple signature or safety guarantee.
