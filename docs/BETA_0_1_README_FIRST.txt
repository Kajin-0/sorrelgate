SORRELGATE BETA 0.1.0 - READ ME FIRST
=====================================

This is a supervised early Mac test, not a finished product. All included
PDFs and their names are fictional. They are NOT genuine contracts.

INSTALLATION
The application is unsigned and not notarized by Apple. Please do NOT
try to treat the downloaded ZIP as a normal self-installing Mac app.
With your permission, a technician or Codex can build and open Sorrelgate
on your Mac. You do not need to type any terminal commands.

Never disable Mac security safeguards, and do not import private customer
documents during this first test.

WHAT TO DO AFTER SORRELGATE OPENS

1. Create an Accepted Offer - Seller Side transaction for the fictional
   address "123 Example Lane".
2. Inspect the starter checklist and mark one item complete.
3. Import all three PDFs from the "Sample Documents" folder.
4. Select a PDF, inspect its original pages, and link it to a checklist item.
5. In the first PDF, use "Find in Selected PDF" under Key Facts Review.
   Open the source page for the date and price; mark one proposal Unknown.
6. In the second PDF, discover the deliberately different purchase price
   and closing date. These are differences to review, not legal findings.
7. Export the transaction into a folder you choose.
8. Quit and reopen Sorrelgate; confirm the transaction, PDFs and tasks remain.

Fill out "TEST RESULTS.txt" with PASS/FAIL and confusing steps.

NOT PRESENT IN THIS BETA
No AI agent, automatic legal checks, timeline generator, drafted emails,
automatic form classification or external email sending. The current
date/price recognizer accepts only narrow explicitly labeled examples.
A person must decide what is trustworthy.

If anything fails, stop that step and record the failure. Use no real
client data until the owner authorizes a separate secured field pilot.
